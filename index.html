HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1. The computer will speak a greeting in a foreign language. Listen carefully!
  2. 2. Think about which country speaks that language.
  3. 3. Answer by clicking the Microphone and speaking, OR type the country name in the input box.
  4. 4. Submit your answer in English.
  5. 5. If you are correct, you advance to the next round!
